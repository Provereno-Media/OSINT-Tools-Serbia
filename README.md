# OSINT-Tools-Serbia
<img src="https://flagcdn.com/w320/rs.png" alt="Serbia_FLAG"/>
A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Serbia. Most of the resources and tools has versions in Serbian (Cyrillic and Latin script) and English. Many register search forms (APR, Tax Administration, NBS, RGZ) work only in Serbian. Several official registers (APR, court portal, RATEL) block automated access, so open them in a normal browser. Public cadastre access shows only basic data on rights holders. Enter names with Serbian diacritics (š, č, ć, đ, ž).

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [Portal otvorenih podataka (Open Data Portal of the Republic of Serbia)](https://data.gov.rs/) — national open data catalogue with about 3,500 datasets from 200+ publishers. Search by keyword, topic or organization. Has an API. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [Republički zavod za statistiku (Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia)](https://www.stat.gov.rs/en-us/) — official statistics, with a statistical database, an open-data portal (CSV/JSON), Census 2022 results, publications and a GeoCensus portal. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Narodna banka Srbije — Statistika (National Bank of Serbia — Statistics)](https://www.nbs.rs/en/drugi-nivo-navigacije/statistika/) — monetary, external-sector, fiscal and payment-system statistics. Also balance sheets of leasing, factoring and investment-fund sectors and historical SEE data. Mostly XLS downloads. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Baza sudske prakse Vrhovnog suda (Supreme Court Court-Practice Database)](https://www.vrh.sud.rs/en/court-practice-database) — selected decisions of the Supreme Court and the former Supreme Court of Cassation, with advanced search. Links to Constitutional Court and ECHR decisions. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Portal sudova Srbije (Portal of Serbian Courts)](https://portal.sud.rs/sr) — online case lookup for Serbian courts (linked from the Supreme Court site as "Online Case Lookup"). *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [Pravno-informacioni sistem RS (Legal Information System of Serbia)](https://www.pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/) — electronic collection of current laws and regulations with the Official Gazette (Službeni glasnik) archive. Needs JavaScript. *Lang: SR.*
- [Beogradska berza (Belgrade Stock Exchange)](https://bgdx.rs/eng/) — daily trading reports, BELEX indices, listings, market capitalisation, and issuer disclosures such as annual and half-year reports and insider notices. *Lang: SR, EN.*
- [Otvoreni parlament (Open Parliament)](https://otvoreniparlament.rs/) — parliament-monitoring platform with plenary stenographic notes, citizen questions to named MPs and the parliament structure. *Lang: SR.*

## Legal Entities
- [Agencija za privredne registre — Pretrage (Serbian Business Registers Agency — Searches)](https://apr.gov.rs/searches.4090.html) — hub for all free APR searches: companies, entrepreneurs, financial statements, pledges, media, associations, foundations, bankruptcy estates, injunctions, leasing, construction permits, beneficial owners and more. Search databases are in Serbian only. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [APR — Pretraga privrednih društava (APR Company Search)](https://www.apr.gov.rs/registri/privredna-drustva/pretraga-podataka.2022.html) — company register data. Free, no account needed. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [APR — Registar finansijskih izveštaja (APR Register of Financial Statements)](https://fin.apr.gov.rs) — annual financial statements of companies, free to download. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [APR — Centralna evidencija stvarnih vlasnika (APR Central Records of Beneficial Owners)](https://www.apr.gov.rs/registers/the-central-records-of-beneficial-owners/about-central-records-.4082.html) — public central register of natural persons who are beneficial owners of registered entities (since 2018). The search is in the APR Searches hub. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [APR — Registar zaloge (APR Pledge Register)](https://www.apr.gov.rs/registers/pledges/data-search.1735.html) — search of registered pledges on movable assets and rights. Search by pledger (name, JMBG or registration number), pledged asset (registration, chassis or serial number), owner or filing number. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [APR — Registar mera i radnji obezbeđenja (APR Register of Injunctions)](https://www.apr.gov.rs/registers/register-of-injunctions/data-search.1752.html) — court-ordered security measures. Search by debtor, property (cadastral parcel, vehicle registration or chassis number), court decision number or application number. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [APR — Registar stečajnih masa (APR Bankruptcy Estate Register)](https://www.apr.gov.rs/registers/bankruptcy-estate/about-register.1637.html) — central electronic register of bankruptcy estates (since 2010). The search is in the APR Searches hub. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [NBS — Jedinstveni registar računa (NBS Single Register of Accounts)](https://webappcenter.nbs.rs/PnWebApp/CompanyAccount/CompanyAccountResident/Index?isSearchExecuted=false) — bank accounts of resident legal entities and entrepreneurs. Search by account number, registration number, PIB or name and place. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [NBS — Dužnici u prinudnoj naplati (NBS Debtors in Enforced Collection)](https://webappcenter.nbs.rs/PnWebApp/EnforcedCollectionDebtor/EnforcedCollectionDebtor/Index?isSearchExecuted=false) — check whether a legal entity or entrepreneur is in enforced collection, by registration number or PIB. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [Zavod za intelektualnu svojinu — e-Registar žigova (Intellectual Property Office — E-Trademark Register)](https://e-reg.zis.gov.rs/regis/search.htm?lang=sr) — national trademarks. Search by mark (wildcards allowed), applicant or holder, representative, application or registration number, Nice or Vienna class. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Poreska uprava — PIB registar (Tax Administration — Tax ID Register)](https://www.purs.gov.rs/pib.html) — basic taxpayer data from the Unified Register of Taxpayers, by PIB. Requires a slider CAPTCHA. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [Poreska uprava — Registar PDV obveznika (Tax Administration — VAT Register)](https://purs.gov.rs/pravna-lica/pdv/registar.html) — check whether a PIB is registered for VAT. Requires a slider CAPTCHA. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [Regulatorno telo za elektronske medije — Registar pružalaca medijskih usluga (REM — Media Service Providers Register)](https://www.rem.rs/en/media-service-providers-register) — licensed TV, radio, cable/IPTV and on-demand providers with legal name, approval number, coverage and validity. Filterable, with XLS export. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Centralni registar hartija od vrednosti — Upisani izdavaoci (Central Securities Depository — Registered Issuers)](https://www.crhov.rs/Home/Index/69) — issuers of securities with registration number, name, ISIN, entry type and date. Search by registration number. *Lang: SR.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [Republički geodetski zavod — eKatastar javni pristup (Republic Geodetic Authority — e-Cadastre Public Access)](https://www.rgz.gov.rs/public-access) — online access to the Real Estate and Land Cadastre. Search by parcel number within a cadastral municipality, or by street address. Shows only basic rights-holder data (name or company and address). *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [RGZ — GeoSrbija (National Spatial Data Infrastructure)](https://www.rgz.gov.rs/en-geo-srbija) — national geoportal combining RGZ, statistics and hydrometeorological data: parcels, buildings, addresses, protected areas and elevation. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [RGZ — Registar cena nepokretnosti (Real Estate Price Register)](https://www.rgz.gov.rs/data-from-the-real-estate-price-register) — free map of individual property sales with type, area, date and price. Shows at most 250 random sales per period and excludes legal-entity-to-legal-entity deals. Detailed reports are paid and for professionals only. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Adresni registar (Address Register, open data)](https://data.gov.rs/sr/datasets/adresni-registar/) — RGZ geometry of all streets and house numbers in Serbia as CSV/GPKG, updated weekly. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [RZS — Registar prostornih jedinica i GIS (Statistical Office — Spatial Units Register and GIS)](https://www.stat.gov.rs/en-us/oblasti/registar-prostornih-jedinica-i-gis/) — administrative and statistical units (municipalities, cadastral municipalities, settlements, enumeration areas, NSTJ regions), with maps and change records. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Beoland GIS (Belgrade Land Development Agency GIS)](https://www.beoland.com/en/plans/gis-beoland.html) — Belgrade-only web GIS: digital cadastral plan, urban plans and their boundaries, the General Regulation Plan, land-development fee zones, the landslide cadastre and building-legalization opinions. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Agencija za bezbednost saobraćaja — Integrisana baza podataka (Road Traffic Safety Agency — Integrated Database)](https://bazaabs.abs.gov.rs/absPortal/) — road-safety portal with traffic-accident data, local databases, indicators, road characteristics and risk analysis. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [Podaci o saobraćajnim nezgodama po policijskim upravama i opštinama (Road Accidents by Police Administration and Municipality)](https://data.gov.rs/sr/datasets/podatsi-o-saobratshajnim-nezgodama-po-politsijskim-upravama-i-opshtinama/) — Ministry of Interior open dataset from 2015 onward with date and time, coordinates, accident type, vehicles involved and casualties. XLSX, updated monthly. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [Udruženje osiguravača Srbije — Usluge za građane (Association of Serbian Insurers — Citizen Services)](https://uos.rs/en/citizen-services/) — links to Information Centre searches on vehicles registered in Serbia involved in accidents (by vehicle, policyholder or party), plus a lookup of domestic correspondents for foreign-registered vehicles. *Lang: SR, EN.*

### Maritime
- [Ministarstvo građevinarstva, saobraćaja i infrastrukture — Lučke kapetanije (Ministry of Construction, Transport and Infrastructure — Inland Navigation and Port Authorities)](https://www.mgsi.gov.rs/en/odsek/department-inland-navigation-services-port-authority) — the 12 regional port authorities (Prahovo to Apatin) that keep the ships register. Useful for knowing where to send vessel-register requests. No online search. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Plovput — Rečni informacioni servisi (Directorate for Inland Waterways — River Information Services)](https://plovput.rs/river-information-services) — Serbian RIS on the Danube, Sava and Tisza: AIS vessel tracking (AIS mandatory since 2014), Notices to Skippers and electronic navigational charts. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [MyShipTracking](https://www.myshiptracking.com/) — free live AIS vessel map with vessel, port and port-call databases. Paid plans exist. Coverage depends on volunteer AIS stations. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [Direktorat civilnog vazduhoplovstva — Registar vazduhoplova (Civil Aviation Directorate — Aircraft Register)](https://cad.gov.rs/strana/24541/%D0%A0%D0%B5%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%80) — information on the public Aircraft Register and on the separate records of ultralight, amateur-built and unmanned aircraft. Links to the electronic register access pages and an extract request form. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live aircraft tracking map with search, filters and aircraft history panels. Includes MLAT positions. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Narodna skupština — Aktuelni saziv (National Assembly — Current Legislature)](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/national-assembly/composition/members-of-parliament/current-legislature.487.html) — current MPs, filterable by name and place of residence, with links to parliamentary groups and parties. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Vlada Republike Srbije — Članovi Vlade (Government of Serbia — Members of Government)](https://www.srbija.gov.rs/sastav/en/10/members-of-government.php) — Prime Minister, deputy PMs, ministers and ministers without portfolio, with photos and biographies. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Republička izborna komisija — Rezultati (Republic Electoral Commission — Results)](https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/542645/rezultati/) — results of parliamentary, provincial and local elections for lists and candidates, down to region, municipality and polling station. *Lang: SR.* 🏛️
- [RATEL — Registar prenesenih brojeva (RATEL Ported Number Registry)](https://prenesibroj.rs/?lang=en) — shows which operator a Serbian mobile or fixed number belongs to at the time of the search. Input format 381 + area code + number. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Yettel — Pretraga imenika (Yettel Public Phone Directory)](https://www.yettel.rs/sr/privatni/usluge/ostale-usluge/pozivi-i-poruke/imenik/pretraga-imenika) — Yettel subscribers who opted in. Search by number, name, street or city. Free. *Lang: SR.*
- [KRIK — Imovina političara (KRIK — Politicians' Assets Database)](https://imovinapoliticara.krik.rs/index1.php) — investigative journalists' profiles of Serbian politicians and their close family: assets, income, past activities and business links. Sortable by asset value and income. *Lang: SR.*
- [Library of Congress — Digitized Belgrade Telephone Directories](https://guides.loc.gov/serbia-telephone-directories/digital-directories) — searchable PDFs of Belgrade directories from 1936, 1941, 1947, 1949 and 1952, listing businesses and private individuals. Search in Cyrillic. *Lang: SR, EN.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [Portal javnih nabavki (Public Procurement Portal)](https://jnportal.ujn.gov.rs/) — national e-procurement portal with procurement plans, notices, award and cancellation decisions, the contracts and framework-agreements registers, complaint decisions, and lists of contracting authorities and bidders. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Kancelarija za javne nabavke (Public Procurement Office)](https://www.ujn.gov.rs/en/) — the regulator: legislation, guidance, the CPV vocabulary and annual reports. Links to the portal. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Ministarstvo za javna ulaganja — Javne nabavke (Ministry for Public Investment — Public Procurement)](https://www.obnova.gov.rs/english/public-procurement) — annual procurement plans (2022–2026), tender notices, clarifications and contract award decisions for hospital, construction and science-infrastructure projects, many funded by the CEB. *Lang: SR, EN.* 🏛️
- [Open Contracting Partnership — Serbia (OpenTender)](https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/139) — bulk OCDS data on Serbian procurement from the national portal and TED (07/2020–05/2022) in JSON, CSV and Excel. *Lang: EN.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/search/result) — EU procurement-notice search. Use it for notices concerning Serbian buyers or places of performance. *Lang: EN, multi.*

## WHOIS
- [RNIDS WHOIS (Serbian National Internet Domain Registry)](https://www.rnids.rs/en/whois) — official WHOIS for .rs, .co.rs, .org.rs, .in.rs, .edu.rs and .срб domains. *Lang: SR, EN.*
- [RNIDS — Spisak ovlašćenih registara (RNIDS — Accredited Registrars List)](https://www.rnids.rs/en/%D1%81%D0%BF%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%BA-%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%88%D1%9B%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%85-%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B3%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B0) — about 38 registrars of .rs/.срб domains with websites and support contacts. Useful for identifying who registered a domain. *Lang: SR, EN.*
- [RNIDS — Domenski sporovi (RNIDS — Domain Name Disputes)](https://www.rnids.rs/en/domain-name-disputes) — ADR procedure for .rs/.срб disputes before a Committee at the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Serbia, with links to decisions and ongoing cases. Decisions are on the Serbian version only. *Lang: SR, EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
- [DNSDumpster](https://dnsdumpster.com/) — free domain research tool that maps hosts, DNS records and subdomains related to a domain. *Lang: EN.*
